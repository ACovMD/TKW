The Bra

Our brassiere is engineered specifically for C–H cup sizes. The fit-modifying panels and adjustable strap system provide support that conventional backless options can't deliver for fuller busts. If you wear an H+ cup, please contact us — we're happy to discuss custom options.
Halter-neck, traditional over-the-shoulder, and front-crossed. Each configuration pairs with different necklines and silhouettes — a styling guide is included with every order.
With proper care, our bra is designed for 50+ wears — significantly better value than single-use adhesive alternatives. Adhesive longevity depends on proper application and care.

Sizing & Fit

Measure your shoulder socket circumference — wrap a soft tape around your torso at shoulder-blade level, parallel to the floor — then confirm your cup size. When in doubt, email us and we'll walk you through it personally.
Size up. Our proprietary mesh construction naturally accommodates fluctuations and will conform to your shape over the first few wears.

Application & Care

Start with clean, dry skin — no lotion, oil, or deodorant on the application area. Warm the bra in your hands for 30 seconds to activate the adhesive, position the cups, then press firmly around the entire edge for 30 seconds. A full video tutorial is included with every order.
Hand wash gently with mild soap and warm water after each wear. Never machine wash or dry. Allow to air dry completely and store flat in the original pouch.
